About us

The store PCS never planned to open

ESTUFF started as a hallway argument at PCS. One team wanted to sell the workstations they were already building for themselves. Another had spent a year recording local choirs and wanted a storefront for the albums. The office manager just wanted the candy jar budgeted properly. Instead of choosing, PCS opened a store with three aisles and let each one earn its keep.

A decade later, all three are still here. The hardware aisle pays the rent, the music aisle brings people to the door, and the candy aisle keeps them coming back. We don't pretend there's a grand strategy connecting them — the connection is that we like these things enough to stand behind every one of them.
